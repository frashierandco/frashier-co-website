About the company

Independent by design.

Frashier & Co. is the public-facing trade name of Frashier and Company LLC, an Arizona limited liability company.

We develop and manage our own projects, from a digital collectible-organizing app to useful editorial content for everyday life. Our focus is on straightforward ideas, careful craftsmanship, and making things genuinely useful.

We are based in Arizona, United States, and can be reached directly for company, product, or business inquiries.
